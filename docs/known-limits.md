# Known limits

| Case | What happens | Why |
|---|---|---|
| G49: a real lawyer asks a client to keep a family-court settlement confidential | **False alarm** at sentence 2 | "court" + "case" + "keep it confidential", in scam order and addressed to the listener, is the digital-arrest pattern. Nothing in the first two sentences shows it is genuine. |
| Out-of-scope scams (lottery, job fee, fake relative, electricity cut-off) | Not alarmed | Nazar targets the digital-arrest pattern; these need their own patterns. |
| Language setting does not match the call | Whisper produces garbled text, no alarm | Set the call language, or switch it while listening. |
| All test calls | Written by our team | Results are not real-world accuracy; real call data is the next step. |
