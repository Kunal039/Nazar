How the score works. A digital-arrest scam moves through five steps: Authority, Fear, Isolation, Control, Transfer. Each step has a weight, and the score rises as more steps appear, more so when they come in that order. A genuine line such as "never share your OTP" does not count as a request for an OTP. Alert level is 70.

Honest limits. This is a rule-based prototype tested on scripted calls written by our team, so the results above are not real-world accuracy. It can make mistakes; a family talking about a scam in the news gets a caution. Real phone-call audio on Android needs a dialer or telecom partner, so this demo listens through the laptop microphone.
