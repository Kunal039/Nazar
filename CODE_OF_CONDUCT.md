# Code of Conduct

Nazar exists to protect people, often elderly or first-time smartphone users, from scam calls. We expect the same care in how we work together.

## Our standards
- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to code or to technology.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Give and accept feedback kindly.
- Never share real victims' call recordings, phone numbers or personal details in issues, commits or test data. Test calls must be written by us, not copied from real people.
- Do not use this project to build, test or improve scam scripts.

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, Kunal Mittal (GitHub: @Kunal039). Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributors that break this code.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
